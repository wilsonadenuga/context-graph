Idea: GuidanceGuard – A Guidance Issue Tracking System

A software application that allows DWP colleagues to report guidance or processes that are unclear, outdated, incorrect or contradictory.

Colleagues can submit an issue, which is then assigned to the relevant team for investigation. They can track its progress and see when it has been resolved.

The problem it solves: DWP has extensive operational guidance, and inconsistencies can cause confusion, delays and mistakes. This system would provide a central place to report and track these problems rather than relying on emails or informal communication.

Potential benefit: More accurate guidance, fewer repeated issues, better communication between teams and improved efficiency.
